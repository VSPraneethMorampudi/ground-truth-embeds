Read: the Excel sheet, the GeoJSON file, and KML, CAD and Shapefile sources are read as 0s and 1s. Load: they are stored in the GIS database as two layers, anganwadi_centres (points) and districts (polygons). Serve: the map service publishes the layers and the map draws them. Publish: the finished map, with every centre matched to its district, is published to the portal.
