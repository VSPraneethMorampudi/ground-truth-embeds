# Indian analyst faces

Six fictional Indian character texture edits made with the built-in OpenAI
Image Gen tool, registered to the six Rocketbox head UV layouts.

- `aarav.jpg`: short black hair, light moustache and stubble.
- `meera.jpg`: black bob, full brows, warm brown complexion.
- `kabir.jpg`: wavy black hair, short beard and moustache.
- `ananya.jpg`: tied-back black hair, deeper brown complexion, small maroon bindi.
- `isha.jpg`: shoulder-length black hair and golden-brown complexion.
- `rohan.jpg`: short black hair and a neat moustache.

Each map includes its own dark-brown iris. These represent designed characters,
not portraits of real staff. Exact final prompts are in `prompts.json`.
The source Rocketbox models and original UV layouts are under the MIT notice
in `../LICENSE-rocketbox.txt`.

`manifest.json` stores the measured neck-skin ratios used to match exposed hands
and body skin. The face maps retain their authored color. Package source PNGs
from `output/indian-faces-source/` with `node site/tools/prepare-indian-faces.cjs`.
