v7

Pen for arrows only, one optional Try it per figure

30 Sep 2026 · frozen

Handwriting is kept for the notes that point at something with a pen arrow. Everything else, including the labels in each drawing, the step bar, the Try it bar and the footer, is set in the story's own font, with IDs in mono. Each figure keeps one optional hands-on task where doing it teaches more than watching; the other steps animate. Tasks never block progress, and the stale "next:" cues are gone. Notes sit level with what they point at, and Intake and Projection stay on Chhattisgarh and its neighbouring states: a record that lands far away waits as a pin on the map's edge with its distance.

v6

Your turn: hands-on sketch figures, twice as fast

29 Sep 2026 · frozen

v5's hand-drawn figures with the hands-on feel of v4 brought back. Every step ends in one thing to do: drag the pin home, merge the duplicate, snap the dangle, close the sliver, measure anywhere, zoom yourself, slide the buffer, redraw the boundary, or pick an answer. Solved steps are ticked; "show me" does it for you; autoplay waits, then shows. Animations run about three times faster, and a tap finishes any animation at once.
