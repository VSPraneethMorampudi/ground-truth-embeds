Backend: the Excel sheet of Anganwadi centres and the GeoJSON file of district boundaries are read as binary and stored in a PostGIS database. The map renderer reads PostGIS and draws square map tiles. Frontend: the browser loads the tiles one by one until a street-style map of Chhattisgarh appears, with the Anganwadi centres on top. One centre is followed all the way, from its row in the Excel sheet to its pin on the map.
